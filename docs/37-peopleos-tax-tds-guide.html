1. What this system does

PeopleOS estimates annual income tax on projected salary income, then deducts TDS every month from the payslip so that most of the tax is collected by March.

TermMeaning
CTCCost to company (usually annual). Monthly gross ≈ CTC ÷ 12
Tax regimeNew or Old — different slabs and deductions
Taxable incomeIncome after allowed exemptions and deductions
Annual taxFull-year tax on projected income
TDSTax Deducted at Source — cut from monthly salary
YTDYear to date — totals from April through the selected month
Key idea: Tax is calculated on a yearly projection. The amount taken from salary is monthly.

3. New vs Old tax regime

New regime

Allowed: standard deduction ₹75,000; new slabs; rebate u/s 87A.

Not allowed in this product: Chapter VI-A (80C etc.), professional tax deduction, HRA exemption — all treated as ₹0.

Old regime

Allowed: standard deduction ₹50,000; HRA exemption (Sec 10(13A)); Chapter VI-A / annual deductions; professional tax deduction (max ₹2,500 / year).

5. Annual tax — step by step

  1. Tax base — projected annual / FY salary used for tax.
  2. HRA exemption (Old only)
HRA exempt = minimum of: 1) Actual HRA for the year 2) Rent paid − 10% of (Basic + DA) 3) 50% of (Basic + DA) if Metro, else 40%
  1. Standard deduction — New ₹75,000 / Old ₹50,000 (capped at income after exemptions).
  2. Other deductions (Old only) — Chapter VI-A / annual deductions; PT up to ₹2,500.
  3. Taxable income
Taxable = Gross − HRA exempt − Std ded − Ch. VI-A − PT (rounded to nearest ₹10)
  1. Tax on slabs — sum of (slice × rate%) for each band.
  2. Rebate u/s 87A
    • New: if taxable ≤ ₹12L → rebate up to ₹60,000; if above ₹12L → marginal relief so tax ≤ (taxable − ₹12L).
    • Old: if taxable ≤ ₹5L → rebate up to ₹12,500.
  3. Surcharge (high income): 0% ≤ ₹50L; 10% ₹50L–1Cr; 15% ₹1–2Cr; 25% ₹2–5Cr; above ₹5Cr → 37% Old / 25% New.
  4. Health & education cess = 4% × (tax after rebate + surcharge).
  5. Total annual tax = tax after rebate + surcharge + cess.

6. Monthly TDS — yearly to monthly

Financial year position: April = 1 … March = 12. monthsLeft includes the current payroll month.

Regular TDS

Projected regular = Prior regular pay this FY + This month fixed gross + (monthsLeft − 1) × monthly gross Monthly regular TDS = max(0, (Annual tax on projected regular − prior regular TDS) ÷ monthsLeft)

Extra TDS

On bonus / overtime / incentive / arrears:

Extra TDS = max(0, Tax(regular + extras) − Tax(regular) − prior extra TDS)

Total this month

TDS = Regular TDS + Extra TDS
Mid-year with blank YTD: If brought-forward taxable/TDS is empty and there are no earlier payslip lines, PeopleOS assumes the current CTC rate from April and that TDS was spread evenly. That is why ₹15L New shows about ₹8,125 in both April and November.

7. Worked example — ₹15,00,000 CTC · New regime

ItemAmount
Projected / annual gross₹15,00,000
Standard deduction₹75,000
Taxable income₹14,25,000
Total annual tax (after rebate & cess)≈ ₹97,500
Monthly TDS≈ ₹8,125 (97,500 ÷ 12)

At ₹12,00,000 CTC New, rebate often brings annual tax to ₹0, so monthly TDS is ₹0.

8. Screens & where to look

  • HR → Salary — structure fields, Calculate, payslip, Tax computation table (Ref, Particulars, Rate, Amount, Formula).
  • Employee → IT Declaration / Proofs / Regime — declare investments & rent; upload proofs; request regime change.
  • HR → Tax reviews — approve declarations (syncs into structure), accept/reject proofs, decide regime changes.
  • Employee → IT Statement / YTD — Form 130 preview and month-wise YTD after payroll exists.

9. Testing checklist

  1. ₹12L New → TDS often ₹0 (rebate).
  2. ₹15L New → TDS about ₹8,125 / month.
  3. Old + rent + 80C → compare taxable / TDS vs same CTC under New.
  4. Bonus in a month → extra TDS that month.
  5. April then May Calculate → YTD adds both months.
  6. Tax table under payslip shows % and formulas.

10. Notes & limits

  • Rates and rebates follow the rules coded in PeopleOS. Confirm against the latest Finance Act for statutory filing.
  • Official Form 16 / government returns may still be issued outside this in-app preview.
  • Reimbursements can raise gross without always changing PF the same way.

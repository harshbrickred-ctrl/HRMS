1. What is PeopleOS?

PeopleOS is the company’s HR and workplace app.

Employees use it for everyday needs: leave, attendance, payslips, documents, helpdesk, and company assets (like a laptop).

Managers, HR, and IT use the same app — they just see extra screens for approvals and admin work.

In one line: one login for work life at the company, instead of chasing emails and spreadsheets.

What it is not (for now): a full payroll calculator, performance reviews, training courses, or expenses.

Payslips are PDF files that HR uploads. People download them here. Salary calculation stays in the payroll system (for example greytHR).

2. The big idea

  1. Everyone signs in once and lands on Home.
  2. The left menu always shows Me (self-service).
  3. Extra menu sections appear based on the person’s job — Manager, HR, IT, Leadership, and so on.
  4. There is no “switch portal” step. One place for day-to-day work.

3. What people can do

Every employee (Me)

  • Apply for leave and see balances
  • See attendance and punch in/out when allowed
  • Download documents and payslip PDFs
  • See and request company assets
  • Raise a helpdesk ticket
  • Change password and screen theme

Managers

  • Approve or reject leave for their team
  • Approve or reject attendance corrections
  • Review team leave and attendance

HR

  • Keep employee records and the org structure up to date
  • Manage leave balances and attendance rules
  • Upload documents and payslips
  • See queues of work that is waiting

IT (and HR Head)

  • Track what the company owns (asset register)
  • Issue, return, and transfer assets
  • Handle fault and loss reports
  • Maintenance and asset reports

Security / Admin

  • Decide who gets which role in the system
  • Approve leave at company level when needed

Leadership

  • See live numbers: headcount, people on leave, exceptions, waiting approvals

4. How work flows

Four everyday stories — follow the arrows.

Leave

Employee applies → Manager approves or rejects → Balance updates → Employee gets a notification

Attendance

Punch on the web or a biometric clock → If wrong, ask for a correction → Manager decides → HR can lock the month for payroll

Laptop / asset

Employee requests kit → Approvals → IT issues it → Employee acknowledges → Later: return, transfer, or report damage/loss

Access

Admin assigns a role (e.g. Manager) → Next login shows new menu → Person can start approving or administering

5. Who sees what

Employee

Me only — self-service for leave, attendance, documents, assets, helpdesk.

Manager

Me + Approvals and team time — decides for their team.

HR Executive

Me + People, leave/attendance admin, documents — day-to-day HR.

HR Head

Everything HR has, plus the full Assets area.

IT Admin

Me + Assets and admin tools — owns company equipment.

Security Admin

Me + Users & Roles — controls who can do what.

Leadership

Me + Leadership reports — sees the organisational pulse.

Finance (read)

No separate Finance menu yet. Can read own payslips if given the role.

6. Ready today vs still coming

Ready to show

  • Sign-in & forgot password
  • Leave apply & approve
  • Attendance (web + clocks)
  • HR records & org
  • Documents & payslip PDFs
  • Assets (employee + IT)
  • Helpdesk (employee side)
  • Leadership numbers
  • Mobile attendance

Still thin

  • Some Home / Manager overview widgets
  • HR helpdesk desk screen
  • Admin audit / jobs / settings
  • Email for approvals (in-app works)
  • Mobile beyond attendance

Not soon

  • Full payroll engine
  • Performance / OKR
  • Training (LMS)
  • Expenses
  • WhatsApp / AI agents

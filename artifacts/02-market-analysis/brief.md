# Market Analysis Brief — What We Compete On (and Don’t)

**Audience:** CEO / Product  
**Artifact type:** Quick brief (1–2 minute skim)

## Thesis

India mid-market HRMS is crowded (greytHR, Keka, Darwinbox, Zoho People, etc.). Enterprise suites (Workday-class) are usually overspec for ~500 FTE. **PeopleOS should not compete on statutory payroll depth**; it should compete on ESS/Manager UX, sane workflows, APIs, and audit — with payroll integrated.

## Key takeaways

- **Incumbent greytHR:** strong India payroll/compliance/attendance narrative; SSO/API often packaged as add-ons on public pricing pages (**Vendor / Fact** — verify our contract).
- **Market pain patterns:** complexity, implementation drag, reporting rigidity, support inconsistency, modular cost surprises (**Observed pattern**, not universal).
- **Table stakes:** employee master, leave, attendance basics, ESS, approvals, documents, RBAC.
- **Build:** Core HR + leave + attendance ops + workflow + documents + ESS/Manager + audit + reports + migration.
- **Integrate / keep:** Indian statutory payroll (greytHR now); later e-sign, BGV, BI.
- **Avoid:** full payroll engine, AI agents Day 1, Darwinbox-scale config studio, microservices.
- **Future SaaS wedge:** modern people SoR + workflow + APIs for India mid-market with payroll partners.

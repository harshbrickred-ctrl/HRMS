What This Product Is About

Purpose

PeopleOS is an owned digital people platform for the company. It becomes the system of record for employee and organization master data, leave and attendance coordination, approval workflows, HR documents, employee and manager self-service, and operational workforce reporting.

It is designed so that, after successful internal use, the same platform can evolve into a commercial multi-tenant SaaS product without a rewrite — while the first 12 weeks deliberately do not fund commercial SaaS features.

The problem it addresses

The company already runs greytHR Fact. Day-to-day friction is believed to remain in manager approvals, attendance corrections, document control, leadership reporting, and process changes that wait on vendor configuration — often spilling into email and Excel Assumption — validate Week 1.

Management Ask / Recommendation

Approve with conditions

Conditions — non-negotiable

  1. P0 scope freeze as listed in Section 4; P1 only with Lead confirmation of spare capacity.
  2. No statutory payroll engine in the 12-week program.
  3. greytHR remains payroll system of record until an explicit Phase 2 decision.
  4. Azure spend gated on UAT pass + pilot adoption thresholds, defined in Weeks 1–2.
  5. Security review + backup/restore drill before company-wide rollout.
  6. Weekly demos to HR Head; scope changes require written trade-offs.
  7. Success metrics baselined in Weeks 1–2; 90-day review with CEO.

Metrics to measure no invented ROI

AreaMetric to measure
Adoption% employees monthly active on ESS
Self-service% leave requests in-system (not email)
SpeedMedian leave approval time
HR loadHours/week on Excel consolidations / corrections
Quality% employees with complete mandatory profile fields
TrustSensitive actions with audit events; Sev-1 incidents
LeadershipTime to produce monthly headcount/attrition pack
If conditions are rejected Do not approve a full platform build. Instead run a short discovery and buy/switch evaluation — deepen greytHR vs. alternatives — without engineering a new system of record.
